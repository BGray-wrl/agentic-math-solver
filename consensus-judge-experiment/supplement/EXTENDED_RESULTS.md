Requiring unanimity across a single model's own three runs raises precision for the
recall-biased judges (GPT-OSS 0.716→0.783, Gemma 0.640→0.680) and gives GPT-OSS its best
F1 (0.810). It does not help the already-balanced DeepSeek.

## E5. Provider mix (multi-seed runs)

Per-judge breakdown of which OpenRouter backend served each call (`ext_provider_mix.csv`).
The open-weight judges are routed across many providers — e.g. DeepSeek-V4-Flash spread over
Alibaba, StreamLake, GMICloud, AtlasCloud, Novita, SiliconFlow, and Parasail — while Gemma
is ~93% served by a single provider (Chutes), which is likely why its run-to-run variance is the
smallest (Table 3). We believe this multi-provider routing, at heterogeneous quantizations, is the
source of the cheap tier's run-to-run drift (paper §6.2).

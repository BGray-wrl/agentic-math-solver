
# Three Benchmarks from Deepmind

## AnswerBench
Simple, easily verifiable question-answer pairs for IMO-level questions. (Think 'Answer is 4' or 'Answer is the Null Set')

## GradingBench
Comphrensive dataset of prompts, answers, and human evaluatoion grades 1-7 designed to let people test the quality of the Grader 

## ProofBench
A list of 60 question-proof pairs for IMO-level questions.

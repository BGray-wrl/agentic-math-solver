In the study of Invariant Theory and Algebraic Reconstruction, consider the following problem statement regarding the properties of Zariski-generic matrix tensors.
Let $n \geq 5$.  
Let $A^{(1)}, \ldots, A^{(n)} \in \mathbb{R}^{3 \times 4}$ be Zariski-generic.   

For $\alpha, \beta, \gamma, \delta \in [n]$, construct $Q^{(\alpha \beta \gamma \delta)} \in \mathbb{R}^{3 \times 3 \times 3 \times 3}$ as the determinant of the $4 \times 4$ matrix formed by the $i$-th row of $A^{(\alpha)}$, the $j$-th row of $A^{(\beta)}$, the $k$-th row of $A^{(\gamma)}$, and the $\ell$-th row of $A^{(\delta)}$ for $1 \leq i, j, k, \ell \leq 3$.

We are interested in algebraic relations on the set of tensors $\{Q^{(\alpha \beta \gamma \delta)} : \alpha, \beta, \gamma, \delta \in [n] \}$.

More precisely, does there exist a polynomial map $\mathbf{F}: \mathbb{R}^{81n^4} \rightarrow \mathbb{R}^N$ that satisfies the following three properties?

The map $\mathbf{F}$ does not depend on $A^{(1)}, \ldots A^{(n)}$. 
The degrees of the coordinate functions of $\mathbf{F}$ do not depend on $n$.
Let $C \in \mathbb{R}^{n \times n \times n \times n}$ satisfy $C_{\alpha \beta \gamma \delta} \neq 0$ for precisely $\alpha, \beta, \gamma, \delta \in [n]$ that are not identical.  Then $\mathbf{F}(C_{\alpha \beta \gamma \delta} Q^{(\alpha \beta \gamma \delta)} : \alpha, \beta, \gamma, \delta \in [n]) = 0$ holds if and only if there exist $u, v, w, x \in (\mathbb{R}^*)^n$ such that $C_{\alpha \beta \gamma \delta} = u_{\alpha} v_{\beta} w_{\gamma} x_{\delta}$ for all $\alpha, \beta, \gamma, \delta \in [n]$ that are not identical.

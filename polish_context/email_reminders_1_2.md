Dear authors,

Thank you for submitting your work to the Evaluations and Datasets track. We would like to share a few important reminders.

Reminder on LaTeX submission format (dataset submissions) We reiterate a previous clarification regarding the LaTeX format for ED submissions. Previously, authors of dataset submissions where anonymisation is not possible for scientific or ethical reasons (e.g., due to dataset constraints) could opt for a single-blind format using:
\usepackage[eandd, nonanonymous]{neurips_2026}

However, this option reduces the space available for the main content. To ensure fair and consistent use of space across all submissions, we ask that all papers use the default double-blind format:

\usepackage[eandd]{neurips_2026}

Authors whose dataset submissions cannot be fully anonymised (e.g., due to dataset links, hosting platforms, or collection processes revealing institutional affiliation) should instead indicate their review policy as single-blind in the submission form using the dedicated field. This will be sufficient to justify any non-anonymized information.

If you have already used the nonanonymous option, please edit as above and recompile your paper. Authors who missed this information will not have their submissions affected during review.

Responsible AI metadata tools (dataset submissions)
Authors with dataset-centered submissions must include Responsible AI (RAI) metadata in the Croissant file. To support this, we provide:

An online RAI editor to help complete the required RAI metadata fields
https://huggingface.co/spaces/JoaquinVanschoren/croissant-rai-checker

A Croissant validator to ensure files are complete and correctly formatted
https://huggingface.co/spaces/JoaquinVanschoren/croissant-checker

We encourage authors to use these tools to ensure compliance.

Anonymity requirements
All double-blind submissions (as indicated in OpenReview) must be fully anonymized, including any dataset URLs and code repository URLs. I.e., the author’s identity must not be obvious from these URLs. For dataset submissions where anonymization is not possible, authors should select a single-blind review policy in the submission form, which permits inclusion of non-anonymized resources.

For full details on the dataset hosting and submission requirements, please see:

the Call for Papers: https://neurips.cc/Conferences/2026/CallForEvaluationsDatasets

the data hosting guidelines; https://neurips.cc/Conferences/2026/EvaluationsDatasetsHosting

our FAQ https://neurips.cc/Conferences/2026/EvaluationsDatasetsFAQ

Sincerely,

NeurIPS 2026 ED Chairs

Dear authors,

Thank you for submitting your abstract to the NeurIPS 2026 ED track. As we quickly approach the paper submission deadline, we would like to bring to your attention some important guidelines as below:

The paper submission deadline is May 6, 23:59 AOE. We strongly encourage you to submit a full draft of your paper well in advance of this deadline in case you encounter slow network issues and other unexpected challenges to upload your files. Please note that this deadline is firm and all paper submissions without a pdf file will be desk-rejected.

You can find important information about paper submission in the Handbook: https://neurips.cc/Conferences/2026/MainTrackHandbook

Papers should be formatted using the NeurIPS 2026 style files. There is a strict upper limit of 9 pages for the main text, with unlimited additional pages for references and appendices. Submissions that do not adhere to the NeurIPS 2026 style files or exceed the page limit will be desk-rejected.

When you upload the paper in OpenReview, please include any appendices in the main PDF, after the references. Appendices should NOT be included in the supplementary material.

Please make sure that emails from noreply@openreview.net do not go to your spam folder. We will be using this address to contact you about your submission.

We have been receiving many questions related to submissions, authorship, and reciprocal reviewing. Unless you have truly exceptional/novel circumstances, we cannot make exceptions to what is already stated. Please see the Handbook: https://neurips.cc/Conferences/2026/MainTrackHandbook

Please take a moment to verify that your OpenReview profile is fully up to date, including your latest publications and current affiliations. This data is critical for our matching algorithm.

We also encourage you to remind yourself of our detailed information provided in the:

Call for Papers: https://neurips.cc/Conferences/2026/CallForEvaluationsDatasets

Hosting guidelines: https://neurips.cc/Conferences/2026/EvaluationsDatasetsHosting

FAQs: https://neurips.cc/Conferences/2026/EvaluationsDatasetsFAQ

Good luck with your submissions! Feel free to reach us at evaluationsdatasets@neurips.cc if you have questions that are not addressed above!
